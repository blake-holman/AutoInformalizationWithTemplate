We will show that the sum of two even natural numbers \( a \) and \( b \) is also even. Assume there exist natural numbers \( r_1 \) and \( r_2 \) such that \( a = r_1 + r_1 \) and \( b = r_2 + r_2 \). To prove the sum \( a + b \) is even, it suffices to demonstrate that \( a + b = r_1 + r_2 + (r_1 + r_2) \). Using the commutativity and associativity of addition, we rewrite \( a + b = r_1 + r_1 + r_2 + r_2 \), which, by regrouping, equals \( r_1 + r_2 + (r_1 + r_2) \). Thus, since \( a + b \) can be expressed as twice the natural number \( r_1 + r_2 \), the sum \( a + b \) is even, completing the proof.
