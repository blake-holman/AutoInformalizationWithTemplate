We will show that \( A \), \( B \), \( C \), \( D \), and \( E \) all hold. Starting from the assumption that \( B \), \( C \), \( D \), \( A \), and \( E \) all hold, we use the commutativity and associativity of conjunction to rearrange the goal \( A \wedge B \wedge C \wedge D \wedge E \) into equivalent forms such as \( B \wedge C \wedge D \wedge E \wedge A \) and then \( B \wedge C \wedge D \wedge A \wedge E \). Since all these statements consist of the same conjuncts, and each conjunct is assumed true, the proof is completed by these assumptions.
