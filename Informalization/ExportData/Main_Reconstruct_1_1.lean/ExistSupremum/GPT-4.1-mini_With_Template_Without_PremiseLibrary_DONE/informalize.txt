[answer]
From the introduction rule for existential quantifiers, in order to establish that there exists an element of \( A \) greater than \( x \), it suffices to prove that the specific element \( z \) belongs to \( A \) and is greater than \( x \).

The proof is completed from the assumption that any number \( z \) between \( x \) and \( c \) belongs to the set \( A \) and the assumption that \( z \) lies between \( x \) and \( c \).

[answer]
Since the assumption that every element of \( A \) is less than or equal to \( x \) conflicts with the existence of an element in \( A \) greater than \( x \), this leads to a contradiction. Therefore, we complete the proof.
