We will show that for any element \( s \) in a nonempty set \( S \subseteq \mathbb{R} \) that is bounded below, \( s \) is greater than or equal to the greatest lower bound (infimum) of \( S \). By the definition of the greatest lower bound, the infimum of \( S \) is itself a lower bound of the set, meaning it is less than or equal to every element in \( S \). Since \( s \) is an arbitrary element of \( S \), it follows that \( s \geq \inf S \), establishing the desired result.
