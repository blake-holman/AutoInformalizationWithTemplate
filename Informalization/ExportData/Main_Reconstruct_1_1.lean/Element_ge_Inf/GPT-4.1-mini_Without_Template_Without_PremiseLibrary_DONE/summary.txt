We will show that any element \( s \) of a set \( S \) is greater than or equal to the greatest lower bound of \( S \). To begin, we note that by the definition of the greatest lower bound, it serves as a lower bound for \( S \); that is, it is less than or equal to every element in \( S \). Since \( s \) is an arbitrary element of \( S \), it follows directly from this property that \( s \) is greater than or equal to the greatest lower bound of \( S \). Hence, the statement is established.
