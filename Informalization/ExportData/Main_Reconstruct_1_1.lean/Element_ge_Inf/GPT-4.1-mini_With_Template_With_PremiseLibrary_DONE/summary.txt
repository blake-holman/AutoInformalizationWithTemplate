We will show that every element \( s \) of a nonempty set \( S \subseteq \mathbb{R} \) that is bounded below satisfies \( s \geq \inf S \). By the definition of the infimum, \(\inf S\) is the greatest lower bound of \( S \), which implies that \(\inf S\) is itself a lower bound of \( S \). Since \( s \in S \) and \(\inf S\) is a lower bound, it follows directly that \( s \geq \inf S \). Thus, the proposition is established.
