[answer]
We will show that no natural number belongs to the set \( S \) by using strong induction on \( n \). Assume, for sake of contradiction, that there exists a natural number \( n \) such that \( n \in S \). By the given property of the set \( S \), there must exist an element \( y \in S \) with \( y < n \). Since \( y < n \), the induction hypothesis applies and tells us that \( y \notin S \). However, this contradicts the fact that \( y \in S \). Therefore, our assumption that \( n \in S \) must be false. Hence, no natural number belongs to the set \( S \), completing the proof.
