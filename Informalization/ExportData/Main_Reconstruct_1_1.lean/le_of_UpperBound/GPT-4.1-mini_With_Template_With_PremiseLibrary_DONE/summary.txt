We will show that if \( a \) is an upper bound of a set \( S \), then any real number \( s \) with \( s \geq a \) is also an upper bound of \( S \). Assume \( s \geq a \) and let \( b \in S \). Since \( a \) is an upper bound of \( S \), it follows by definition that \( b \leq a \). Combining this with the assumption \( s \geq a \), we conclude that \( b \leq s \). Hence, \( s \) is also an upper bound of the set \( S \), completing the proof.
