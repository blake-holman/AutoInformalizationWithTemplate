have : b ≤ a := by
      apply ub;exact  binS 

[answer]
We will show that \( b \leq a \). Since \( a \) is assumed to be an upper bound of the set \( S \), it suffices to prove that \( b \in S \) to conclude that \( b \leq a \). Given that \( b \) indeed belongs to the set \( S \) by assumption, it follows directly that \( b \leq a \), completing the proof.
