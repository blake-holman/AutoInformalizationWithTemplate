We will show that for any real number \( s \) such that \( s \geq a \), and for any element \( b \in S \), it holds that \( b \leq s \). Since \( a \) is an upper bound of the set \( S \), every element \( b \in S \) satisfies \( b \leq a \). Given that \( s \geq a \), we then have \( b \leq a \leq s \), which implies \( b \leq s \). This completes the proof.
