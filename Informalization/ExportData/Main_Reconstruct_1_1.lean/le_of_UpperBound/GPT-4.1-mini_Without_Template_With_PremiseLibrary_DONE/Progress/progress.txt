have : b ≤ a := by
      apply ub;exact  binS 

[answer]
We will show that \( b \leq a \) by leveraging the fact that \( a \) is an upper bound of the set \( S \). Since \( a \) is an upper bound, it suffices to verify that \( b \in S \). Given that \( b \) indeed belongs to \( S \), it follows immediately that \( b \leq a \), completing the proof.
