We will show that for any real number \( s \) with \( s \geq a \) and for any element \( b \in S \), it holds that \( b \leq s \). First, we observe that to prove \( b \leq a \), it suffices to use the fact that \( a \) is an upper bound of the set \( S \). Since \( b \) is assumed to belong to \( S \), the definition of upper bound immediately gives \( b \leq a \). Finally, given that \( s \geq a \), it follows by transitivity that \( b \leq s \), completing the proof.
