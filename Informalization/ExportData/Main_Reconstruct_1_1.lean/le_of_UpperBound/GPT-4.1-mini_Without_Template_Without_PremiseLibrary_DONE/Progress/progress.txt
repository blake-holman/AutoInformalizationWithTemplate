[answer]
We will show that \( b \leq a \) by leveraging the fact that \( a \) is an upper bound of the set \( S \). Since \( a \) is an upper bound, any element of \( S \) must be less than or equal to \( a \). Therefore, to establish the desired inequality, it suffices to verify that \( b \in S \). Given that \( b \) is indeed an element of \( S \), it follows immediately that \( b \leq a \), completing the proof.
