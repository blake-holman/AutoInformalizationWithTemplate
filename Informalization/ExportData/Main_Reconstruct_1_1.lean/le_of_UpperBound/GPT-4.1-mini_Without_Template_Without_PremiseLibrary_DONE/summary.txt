We will show that for any real number \( s \) with \( s \geq a \) and any element \( b \) of the set \( S \), it holds that \( b \leq s \). Since \( a \) is an upper bound of the set \( S \), every element \( b \in S \) satisfies \( b \leq a \). Given that \( b \in S \), this directly implies \( b \leq a \). Finally, because \( s \geq a \), the transitivity of the inequality gives \( b \leq s \), completing the proof.
