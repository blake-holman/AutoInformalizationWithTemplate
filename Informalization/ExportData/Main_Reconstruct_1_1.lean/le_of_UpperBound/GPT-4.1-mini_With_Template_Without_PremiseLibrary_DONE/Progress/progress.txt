have : b ≤ a := by
      apply ub;exact  binS 

[answer]
We will show that \( b \leq a \) by using the fact that \( a \) is an upper bound of the set \( S \). By definition, since \( a \) is an upper bound of \( S \), every element of \( S \) is less than or equal to \( a \). Therefore, to establish \( b \leq a \), it suffices to verify that \( b \in S \). Given the assumption that \( b \) is indeed an element of \( S \), it follows directly that \( b \leq a \). Hence, the proposition is proved.
