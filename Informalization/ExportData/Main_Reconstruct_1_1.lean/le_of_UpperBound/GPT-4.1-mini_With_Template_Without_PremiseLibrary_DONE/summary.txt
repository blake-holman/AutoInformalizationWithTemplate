We will show that for any real number \( s \) with \( s \geq a \) and any element \( b \) of the set \( S \), it holds that \( b \leq s \). Since \( a \) is an upper bound of \( S \), by definition every element \( b \in S \) satisfies \( b \leq a \). Given our assumption that \( b \in S \), it follows immediately that \( b \leq a \). Finally, from the inequalities \( b \leq a \) and \( s \geq a \), we conclude \( b \leq s \), thus completing the proof.
