We will show that if \(a\) and \(b\) are both infimums of the set \(S\), then \(a = b\). By the definition of infimum, both \(a\) and \(b\) are lower bounds of \(S\), and each is greater than or equal to every other lower bound of \(S\). Since \(a\) is less than or equal to any lower bound, in particular \(a \leq b\), and similarly, since \(b\) is also an infimum, \(b \leq a\). Therefore, by the antisymmetry property of the real numbers, it follows that \(a = b\), completing the proof.
