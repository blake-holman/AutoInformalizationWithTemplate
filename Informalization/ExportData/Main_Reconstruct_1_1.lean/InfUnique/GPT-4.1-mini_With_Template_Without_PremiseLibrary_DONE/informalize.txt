[appliedTactic]
intro aInf  bInf 
[answer]
Assume that \( a \) and \( b \) are both infimums of the set \( S \). From now on, we will show that \( a = b \).
[appliedTactic]
rw [Infimum] at  * 
[answer]
By using the definition of the greatest lower bound, the assumption that \( a \) is the infimum of the set \( S \) becomes the statement that \( a \) is a lower bound of \( S \) and that any other lower bound of \( S \) is less than or equal to \( a \), and similarly for \( b \); the goal of proving \( a = b \) remains unchanged.
[appliedTactic]
apply le_antisymm (bInf.right a aInf.left) (aInf.right b bInf.left ) 
[answer]
From the antisymmetry property of the order relation and the assumptions that \( a \) and \( b \) are both lower bounds of the set \( S \) with each being less than or equal to any other lower bound, in order to establish that \( a = b \), it suffices to prove that \( a \leq b \) and \( b \leq a \).
