We will show that if for every positive number \( \varepsilon \), the absolute value of \( a \) satisfies \( |a| < \varepsilon \), then \( a = 0 \). First, since \( |a| < \varepsilon \) holds for every positive \( \varepsilon \), it follows that \( |a| \) is less than any positive number arbitrarily close to zero. By the principle that if a quantity is less than every positive number, then it must be less than or equal to zero, we conclude \( |a| \leq 0 \). Since the absolute value is always nonnegative, this forces \( |a| = 0 \), which implies \( a = 0 \). Hence, the proof is complete.
