We will show that if the absolute value of a real number \( a \) is less than every positive number \( \varepsilon \), then \( a = 0 \). Assuming that for every \( \varepsilon > 0 \), we have \(|a| < \varepsilon\), it follows by the principle that a real number less than every positive number must be less than or equal to zero that \(|a| \leq 0\). Since the absolute value is always nonnegative, this implies \(|a| = 0\), and consequently, by the fundamental property of absolute value, \( a = 0 \). Thus, the proof is complete.
