We will show that if for every positive number \(\varepsilon\), the absolute value of \(a\) satisfies \(|a| < \varepsilon\), then \(a = 0\). First, note that the hypothesis states \(|a|\) is less than every positive real number, no matter how small. By the property of real numbers, if \(|a|\) is less than every positive number, it must be less than or equal to zero, hence \(|a| \leq 0\). Since absolute values are always nonnegative, the only possibility is \(|a| = 0\). Finally, by the definition of absolute value, this implies \(a = 0\), completing the proof.
