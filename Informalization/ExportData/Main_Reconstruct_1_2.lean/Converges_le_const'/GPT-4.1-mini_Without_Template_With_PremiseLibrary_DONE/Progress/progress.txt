[applied Tactic]
have xnleyn : ∀n, x n ≤ y n := by
      intro n
      exact xnleb  n 

[answer]
We will show that for every natural number \( n \), the value of the sequence \( x \) at \( n \) is less than or equal to the value of the sequence \( y \) at \( n \). To this end, let \( n \) be an arbitrary natural number. By the given inequality, we have \( x(n) \leq b \). Since the sequence \( y \) is constant with value \( b \) at every natural number \( n \), it follows that \( y(n) = b \). Therefore, for this arbitrary \( n \), \( x(n) \leq y(n) \). Because \( n \) was chosen arbitrarily, the inequality holds for all natural numbers \( n \), completing the proof.
