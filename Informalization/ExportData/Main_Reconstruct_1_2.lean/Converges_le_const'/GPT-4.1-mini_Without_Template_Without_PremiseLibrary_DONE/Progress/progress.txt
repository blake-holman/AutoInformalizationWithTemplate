have xnleyn : ∀n, x n ≤ y n := by
      intro n
      exact xnleb  n 

[answer]
We will show that for every natural number \( n \), the value of the sequence \( x \) at \( n \) is less than or equal to the value of the sequence \( y \) at \( n \). To this end, let \( n \) be an arbitrary natural number. By the given assumption, for every such \( n \), the value of the sequence \( x \) at \( n \) is less than or equal to some constant \( b \). Since the sequence \( y \) is defined as the constant sequence with value \( b \), it follows directly that \( x(n) \leq y(n) \). This completes the proof.
