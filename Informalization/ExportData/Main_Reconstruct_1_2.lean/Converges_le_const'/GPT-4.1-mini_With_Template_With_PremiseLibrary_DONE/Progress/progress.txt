have xnleyn : ∀n, x n ≤ y n := by
      intro n
      exact xnleb  n 

[answer]
We will show that for every natural number \( n \), the value of the sequence \( x(n) \) is less than or equal to \( y(n) \). Assume that \( n \) is a natural number. By the given assumption, for every natural number \( n \), the value \( x(n) \) is less than or equal to the constant \( b \). Since \( y(n) \) is equal to this constant \( b \), it follows directly that \( x(n) \leq y(n) \), completing the proof.
