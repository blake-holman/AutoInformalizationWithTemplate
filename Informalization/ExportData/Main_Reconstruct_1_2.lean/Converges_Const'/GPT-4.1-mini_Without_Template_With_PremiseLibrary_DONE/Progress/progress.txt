[applied Tactic]
have : |(fun _ => a) n - a| = 0 := by
    rw [sub_self,abs_zero ] 

[answer]
We will show that the absolute value of the difference between the constant function evaluated at \( n \) and \( a \) is zero. To see this, observe that subtracting \( a \) from itself yields zero. Since the absolute value of zero is zero, it follows that the expression simplifies to zero, thereby establishing the claim.
