      exact xngeb  n 

[answer]
We will show that for every natural number \( n \), the value of the sequence \( y \) at \( n \) is less than or equal to the value of the sequence \( x \) at \( n \). Assume that \( n \) is an arbitrary natural number. Since \( y \) is the constant sequence equal to \( b \), its value at \( n \) is \( b \). By the given assumption, for every natural number \( n \), the value of the sequence \( x \) at \( n \) is greater than or equal to \( b \). Therefore, it follows that \( y(n) = b \leq x(n) \), completing the proof.
