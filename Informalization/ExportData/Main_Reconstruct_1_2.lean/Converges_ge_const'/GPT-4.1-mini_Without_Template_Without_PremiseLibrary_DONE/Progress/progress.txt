[answer]
We will show that for every natural number \( n \), the value of the constant sequence \( y \) at \( n \) is less than or equal to the value of the sequence \( x \) at \( n \). To this end, let \( n \) be an arbitrary natural number. By the given fact, we know that \( x_n \geq b \) for all \( n \). Since \( y \) is the constant sequence defined by \( b \), it follows that \( y_n = b \leq x_n \). Thus, the inequality holds for every natural number \( n \), completing the proof.
