|x_n y_n - ab| = |(x_n y_n - x_n b) + (x_n b - ab)|,
\]

\[
|x_n y_n - ab| \leq |x_n|\,|y_n - b| + |x_n - a|\,|b|.
\]

\[
|x_n y_n - ab| \leq M |y_n - b| + M |x_n - a|.

\[
M |y_n - b| < \frac{\varepsilon}{2}, \quad M |x_n - a| < \frac{\varepsilon}{2}.
\]
Adding these inequalities yields
\[
|x_n y_n - ab| < \frac{\varepsilon}{2} + \frac{\varepsilon}{2} = \varepsilon.
\]

Since \(\varepsilon > 0\) was arbitrary, this shows that the sequence \(x_n y_n\) converges to \(ab\), completing the proof.
