[applied Tactic]
have xnleyn : ∀n, x n ≤ y n := by
      intro n
      exact xnlea  n 

[answer]
We will show that for every natural number \( n \), the value \( x(n) \) is less than or equal to \( y(n) \). To this end, let \( n \) be an arbitrary natural number. By assumption, for each natural number \( n \), we have \( x(n) \leq a \). Since \( y \) is defined as the constant sequence equal to \( a \), it follows directly that \( x(n) \leq y(n) \). Hence, the desired inequality holds for all natural numbers \( n \), completing the proof.
