[applied Tactic]
have xnleyn : ∀n, x n ≤ y n := by
      intro n
      exact xnlea  n 

[answer]
We will show that for every natural number \( n \), the value of \( x_n \) is less than or equal to the value of \( y_n \). Assume \( n \) is an arbitrary natural number. From the assumption that for every natural number \( n \), \( x(n) \leq a \), it follows that \( x(n) \leq y(n) \) since \( y(n) \) is greater than or equal to \( a \). Hence, the proposition holds for all natural numbers \( n \).
