[answer]
We will show that for every natural number \( n \), the value of \( y(n) \) is less than or equal to the value of \( x(n) \). Assume that \( n \) is arbitrary but fixed. Given the assumption that for every natural number \( n \), the value of \( x(n) \) is greater than or equal to some constant \( a \), it follows directly that \( y(n) \leq x(n) \). Thus, the desired inequality holds for all natural numbers \( n \).
