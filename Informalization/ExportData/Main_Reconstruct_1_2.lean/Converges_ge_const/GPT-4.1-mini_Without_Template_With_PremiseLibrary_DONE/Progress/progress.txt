[applied Tactic]
have ynlexn : ∀n, y n ≤ x n := by
      intro n
      exact xngea  n 

[answer]
We will show that for every natural number \( n \), the value of the constant sequence \( y \) at \( n \) is less than or equal to the value of the sequence \( x \) at \( n \). To this end, let \( n \) be an arbitrary natural number. By the given hypothesis, for every \( n \), the value of the sequence \( x \) at \( n \) is greater than or equal to \( a \), where \( a \) defines the constant value of the sequence \( y \). Therefore, it follows directly that \( y(n) = a \leq x(n) \), completing the proof.
