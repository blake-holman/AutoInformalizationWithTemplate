have ynlexn : ∀n, y n ≤ x n := by
      intro n
      exact xngea  n 

[answer]
We will show that for every natural number \( n \), the value of the function \( y \) at \( n \) is less than or equal to the value of the function \( x \) at \( n \). Let \( n \) be an arbitrary natural number. By the given assumption, we know that \( x(n) \) is always greater than or equal to \( a \). Since the constant sequence defined by \( a \) is therefore less than or equal to \( x(n) \), it follows directly that \( y(n) \leq x(n) \). This completes the proof.
